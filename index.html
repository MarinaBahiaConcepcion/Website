The Vision

Anchor in paradise

On the protected western shore of Bahía Concepción, where the desert meets turquoise water, a private harbor is taking shape. Arrive by sea and step off your yacht onto the dock, or arrive by land and disappear into a cabaña at the water's edge. Swim, sail, dine, and watch the sun set over the Sierra.

The Marina & Resort

  • Private Dock

    Direct boat access from deep water. Tie up steps from the club house.

  • Mooring Buoys

    Protected moorings for sailboats in the calm of the bay, season after season.

  • Club House

    Lounge, restaurant & bar, and an outdoor deck overlooking the water.

  • Pool

    A pool overlooking the bay. Swim with the Sea of Cortés on the horizon.

  • Beach Access

    A private stretch of sand with kayaks and paddle boards at the ready.

  • Ten Cabañas

    Nos. 1 to 5 face the ocean at dawn; Nos. 6 to 10 face the sunset over the Sierra.

  • Fire Pit

    A gathering place under some of the darkest, starriest skies in México.

  • Parking

    Arriving down Highway 1? Secure on-site parking awaits.

The Location
